Questions

Does it upload my data?

No. It reads Health Connect on the device and stores everything locally. There is no account and no server holding your health data.

Does it work with my Fitbit Air, Charge or Pixel Watch?

If the device writes to Health Connect, yes. The confirmed list grows as people try it; the beta is where that gets settled.

Can I import years of history?

Yes — from the Google or Fitbit data export. You point the app at the file.

What happens if I stop paying?

You keep your data and can export everything. Always.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's your data, presented properly.
